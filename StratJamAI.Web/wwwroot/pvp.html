      How to play together

      Quick play finds someone with the same starting clock and increment, and a compatible color preference. For a private game, create a room, send the link to a friend, and both choose Ready. Blue starts with one placement; subsequent turns allow two. Your increment is added after your whole turn.

      Choose one of your nodes, then a highlighted legal endpoint. Close shapes to earn territory points each turn. The server checks every move. Your clock continues if you disconnect; reopening this page in the same browser reconnects to your game.

      After the match, save the history or choose Review with AI. AI suggestions are available during the review.
