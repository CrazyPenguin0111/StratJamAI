What the estimates mean

The AI suggests a line using the selected search time. Depth varies by position; a suggestion is not a guaranteed best move.

Static estimates range from −1 to +1 for the player making the move. Static Δ compares the positions immediately after the suggested and played placements. It is not win probability, points lost, or proof of a mistake. Equivalent moves can differ.
