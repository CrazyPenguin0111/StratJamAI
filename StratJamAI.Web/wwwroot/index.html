How to play

Click one of the moving player's colored nodes, then a highlighted endpoint. A line can extend up to three grid spaces along each axis. Legal moves and captures are checked automatically. Close shapes to enclose territory; your score accumulates over the game. Protected lines have a small ring at their midpoint. The turn indicator shows how many placements remain. Highest total score wins after 120 placements.

Analyse game: enter moves for both colors, or load a saved game. Live suggestions update after each change; use Play suggestion to apply one. Undo removes the latest placement. In Play AI mode, Undo returns to before your latest placement, including the AI replies.

Keyboard: focus the board, move with the arrow keys, and select with Enter or Space. Escape clears your selection.
